# D-0001 — The truck is nfx's Blockbench project under his ids; his build, ported, is the pipeline

**Decision**: The Farmer's Pickup ships from this repo as nfx built it: his project as
saved, his build ported to `devtools/art/build.py` (folder wrapping, the greying of the
painted texels against the factory red, the tailgate's patches duplicated, the profile
measured off the cubes), his mod id `farmpickup` and vehicle id `farmpickup:pickup`, his
recipe. The repo carries Rusty's name for it, `minecraft-farmers-truck`; the version starts
at 1.1.0, past the 1.0.x on nfx's instance.

**Why**: Rusty asked for the repo and for the truck in the pack. The ids are the ones his
instance's world already holds; renaming them would orphan every truck in it for a name.

**Alternatives rejected**: *Renaming to `farmerstruck`* -- see above. *Re-rigging the
model to the Trailblazer's folder names* -- the build's wrapping does the same without
asking the modeller to redo his rig.

**Links**: `devtools/art/build.py`, the Trailblazer's D-0003, the protocol's D-0008.
